# Anamul Hoque

**PhD student, Mechanical and Industrial Engineering, University of Massachusetts Amherst**
THEAM Lab · Advisor: Prof. Sunandita Sarker

I work on microscale additive manufacturing by two-photon direct laser writing, and on the data and software needed to make it autonomous: mining process knowledge from the literature, physics-informed prediction of print failures, and experimental validation on a commercial two-photon printer.

## Research

- **DLW-Bench** — a structured knowledge base of 4,245 process records extracted from 2,173 direct laser writing papers with large language models, each value traceable to a verbatim quote from its source.
- **PrintNet** — a 3D U-Net, conditioned on printer and process settings, that predicts where and how a design is likely to fail before it is printed.
- **Paper** — A. Hoque and S. Sarker, "Toward Autonomous Microscale Manufacturing: Physics-Informed Printability Prediction for Direct Laser Writing," ASME International Manufacturing Science and Engineering Conference (MSEC 2027), submitted. [Supplementary information](https://github.com/anamulumass-stack/dlw-bench-printnet-supplementary)

## Research software

- Topology optimization tools for pneumatic soft actuators and inflatable microstructures
- Python desktop tools for scientific workflows: cardiac image visualization and mold generation, mechanical test analysis, and video-based vibration tracking
